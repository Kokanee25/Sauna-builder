## Dimensions (reference)

| Element | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Upper bench height | 36 in (915 mm) |
| Upper bench depth | 24 in (610 mm) |
| Lower bench height | 18 in (460 mm) |
| Lower bench depth | 18 in (460 mm) |
| Slat gap | ~½ in (10–12 mm) |
| Headroom above upper seat | ≥ 44 in (ceiling 84 in − 36 in = 48 in ✅) |
| Bench length (back wall) | 72 in (full 6 ft) — seats 3 / lie-down |

## Construction notes

- **Frame:** 2×4 supports anchored into wall **backing** (added during
  [framing](../docs/04-framing.md)) and/or legs to the floor.
- **Tops:** 2×2 or 1×4 **clear** cedar/aspen slats, spaced ~½ in for drainage and
  airflow; the gaps also keep the seat cooler.
- **Fasteners:** stainless, **countersunk and plugged** — no exposed metal to
  burn skin.
- **Edges:** round/ease all edges and corners.
- **Open front** under the lower bench so air moves and the floor dries; make the
  upper top **removable** for cleaning if possible.
- **Backrest:** slats mounted **angled off the wall** so the back doesn't touch
  hot paneling and air flows behind.

## Why these heights

- The **upper bench at 36 in** sits in the hottest stratified air just below the
  7 ft ceiling — the hot seat.
- The **lower bench at 18 in** is cooler and doubles as the **step** up to the
  upper bench.
