## Key

- **Heater (kiuas):** wall-mounted near the **door wall / corner**. Maintain the
  manufacturer's clearances to the side walls and benches.
- **Intake vent (▲):** low, immediately **below/behind the heater** — cool air
  enters and rises over the stones.
- **Exhaust vent (▼ ▢):** low on the **wall opposite** the heater, with an
  adjustable wooden slider, giving a **diagonal sweep** of air across the room.
- **Benches:** stacked on the **back wall** (farthest from the door swing).
  Upper 36 in / 24 in deep; lower 18 in / 18 in deep.
- **Door:** 24 in wide on the front wall, **swings outward**, ball-catch only.
- **Walking space:** kept clear from door to benches.

## Clearance reminders

- Benches and bathers stay **outside** the heater's required clearance envelope.
