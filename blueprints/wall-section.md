## Layers (exterior → interior)

| # | Layer | Spec | Purpose |
|---|-------|------|---------|
| 1 | Existing wall / exterior sheathing | — | Backing structure |
| 2 | Stud framing | 2×4 KD-SPF @ 16" OC | Cavity & support |
| 3 | Insulation | Mineral wool **R-13** (R-21+ at ceiling) | Slow heat loss |
| 4 | **Vapor barrier** | **Aluminum foil, foil faces room**, seams overlapped + foil-taped | Block moisture + reflect heat in |
| 5 | Air gap | 1×2 furring, ¼–¾ in | Ventilated drying gap; protects foil |
| 6 | Finish | Cedar **T&G** 1×4/1×6, blind-nailed stainless | Touch surface, bare wood |

## Critical notes

- **Foil faces the room** and is **continuous** across walls and ceiling;
  **ceiling foil laps over wall foil**.
- **Tape every seam** with high-temp aluminum foil tape; seal all penetrations.
- **No polyethylene** anywhere as the barrier — foil only.
- Ceiling section is identical but with **R-21+** insulation.
