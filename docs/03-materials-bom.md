# 03 — Materials & Bill of Materials

A complete shopping list for the **reference 6 × 5 × 7 ft indoor electric build**.
Quantities include a typical waste allowance — confirm against your own cut list.

## 1. Choosing the wood

Sauna wood must resist heat, not bleed sap, stay cool to the touch, and resist
rot. **Softwoods that don't drip resin** and **light hardwoods** are used.

| Species | Use | Why / notes |
|---------|-----|-------------|
| **Western Red Cedar** | Walls, ceiling, benches | Aromatic, rot-resistant, stays cool, low resin — the popular all-rounder (reference choice) |
| **Aspen** | Walls, benches | Very light color, almost no scent/resin, stays cool — great for benches if scent is unwanted |
| **Alder** | Walls, benches | Warm color, soft, low resin |
| **Western Hemlock / Nordic Spruce** | Walls (budget) | Clear hemlock is a cheaper paneling option |
| **Thermally-modified (thermowood)** | Walls/benches | Heat-treated for stability; premium |

**Avoid:** pine and other resinous softwoods for interior surfaces (sap bleeds
when hot); any treated/chemically-finished lumber inside; oily tropical hardwoods
that get scorching to the touch. **Never seal interior surfaces with varnish,
paint, or standard stain** — use only sauna-rated paraffin oil if anything.

> **Framing is different from finish.** The hidden stud frame can be ordinary
> **kiln-dried SPF (spruce-pine-fir) construction lumber** — it's behind
> insulation and the vapor barrier, so resin/appearance don't matter.

## 3. Tools

Power: circular saw or table saw, miter saw, drill/driver, brad/finish nailer +
compressor (or hand-nail), jigsaw, oscillating multi-tool.
Hand/measure: tape, speed square, level (2–4 ft), chalk line, stud finder,
utility knife, caulk gun, clamps, sanding block.
Safety: glasses, hearing protection, dust mask (mineral wool!), gloves.

## 4. Estimating for a different size

For interior surface area (paneling, foil, insulation), compute:

- **Wall area** = perimeter × height = `2 × (W + D) × H`
  - Reference: `2 × (6 + 5) × 7 = 154 ft²`
- **Ceiling area** = `W × D` = `6 × 5 = 30 ft²`
- Add **10–15% waste** for T&G and foil.
