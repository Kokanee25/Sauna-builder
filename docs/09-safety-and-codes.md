# 09 — Safety & Codes

A sauna combines heat, electricity (or fire), moisture, and confined occupants.
The items below are non-negotiable. **Local building and electrical codes always
take precedence** over anything here — verify with your building department.

## 1. The door & egress (life safety)

- **Door swings OUTWARD.** Always. So a person can push out even if someone is
  slumped against it inside.
- **No lock that secures from the outside.** It must be openable from inside at
  all times, with a simple ball-catch or roller catch (no latch that can trap).
- **No high threshold** to trip over; keep the path to the door clear.
- **Tempered (safety) glass only** for any glass in the door or walls.

## 2. Heater safety

- **Follow the heater manual's clearances** to walls, ceiling, benches, and floor
  — exactly. These are the manufacturer's listed safety distances.
- **Guard rail:** install a wooden guard around the heater so bathers cannot
  touch the hot body or fall against it. Keep it outside the heater's required
  clearance.
- **Nothing stored on or near the heater.** Towels, buckets, and clothing away
  from it.
- **Stones:** only heater-rated stones, stacked loosely (see [`docs/06`](06-heater-sizing.md)).
- **Automatic shut-off:** most controllers include a timer/limit; ensure your
  heater has over-temperature protection. Don't defeat it.

## 3. Electrical safety (see [`docs/07`](07-electrical.md))

- **Licensed electrician + permit + inspection** for the heater circuit.
- **GFCI protection** per local code.
- **High-temp wiring** inside the hot room; junction boxes outside.
- **No standard outlets/switches/fixtures** in the hot room.
- **Disconnect** so the heater can be fully isolated for service.

## 4. Wood-burning safety (if applicable)

- **Listed chimney** with correct clearances through walls/roof; spark arrestor.
- **Floor protection / hearth** under and in front of the stove.
- **Wall heat shields** at clearances.
- **Combustion air** supply.
- **CO awareness:** ensure complete combustion and draft; consider a CO alarm in
  the adjacent space (not the hot room).
- Solid-fuel appliance approval/inspection where required.

## 5. Materials & finish safety

- **No paint, varnish, or sealing film** on interior wood — it can off-gas or
  blister. Only sauna-rated **paraffin oil** if anything.
- **No plastic** (vapor barrier, vent trim, fixtures) that can melt/off-gas in the
  hot room — foil and wood instead.
- **No resinous woods** (pine) on touch surfaces — hot sap burns and stains.
- **No treated lumber** inside the hot room.

## 6. User-safety design

- **Bench edges** rounded/eased; **fasteners countersunk and plugged** so no hot
  metal contacts skin.
- **Handrails/handles** of wood, positioned for safe stepping onto the upper
  bench.
- **Thermometer + hygrometer** visible so users monitor conditions.
- **Lighting** bright enough to move safely, guarded so it can't be touched.
- **Slip resistance:** duckboard floor; mop up standing water.

## 7. Occupancy & operation rules (post these)

- Max recommended session length; hydrate; never use alone if unwell.
- No alcohol; supervise children; medical caution (heart conditions, pregnancy).
- Keep the door operable and the path clear.
- Dry the room after use ([`docs/08`](08-ventilation.md)).

## 8. Codes & permits to confirm

| Item | Typically governed by |
|------|------------------------|
| Heater circuit, GFCI, wiring | Electrical code (NEC/CEC/local) + permit + inspection |
| Structure / outbuilding | Building code + permit |
| Wood stove + chimney | Solid-fuel/mechanical code + inspection |
| Outdoor placement | Zoning / setbacks |
| Egress & glass | Building code (safety glazing, door swing) |

**Action:** call your local building department early ([`docs/01`](01-planning.md))
and confirm which apply to your exact project. Don't assume "it's small, so no
permit."

## 9. Safety checklist

- [ ] Door swings out, no outside lock, tempered glass
- [ ] Heater clearances met; guard rail installed
- [ ] Electrical permitted, GFCI, high-temp leads, inspected
- [ ] (Wood) chimney/hearth/shields/combustion air correct & inspected
- [ ] Only sauna-safe woods and finishes; no plastic in hot room
- [ ] Fasteners countersunk/plugged; edges eased; handrails fitted
- [ ] Thermometer/hygrometer installed; guarded lighting
- [ ] Operating rules posted
- [ ] All required permits/inspections closed out
