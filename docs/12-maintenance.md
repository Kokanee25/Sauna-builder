# 12 — Maintenance & First Fire

## 1. Curing / first heat (commissioning)

Before normal use, run a **cure cycle** so the heater elements and stones burn off
manufacturing oils and the wood settles.

1. **Read the heater manual's break-in procedure** and follow it first.
2. Open ventilation **fully** and crack the door.
3. Run the heater to operating temperature for **30–60 minutes with NO water on
   the stones.** Expect some smell/light haze as oils burn off — this is normal;
   ensure the space is ventilated.
4. Let it cool, then run a second cycle. Watch for hot spots, odd smells (resin =
   you used the wrong wood somewhere), buzzing, or controller/sensor errors.
5. Verify the room reaches target (**≈ 175–195 °F / 80–90 °C** at the upper bench)
   and that the **sensor/controller readout matches** an independent thermometer.
6. Only after curing, test **löyly** — ladle a small amount of water on the hot
   stones and confirm a clean steam burst.

## 2. After every session

- **Open the exhaust/drying vent fully and leave the door ajar** until the room is
  completely dry. This is the most important habit for longevity.
- Wipe down benches; remove any duckboard that's wet to dry separately if needed.
- Empty the water bucket.
- Turn off the heater and confirm the controller timer has cut power.

## 3. Weekly / regular

- Wipe benches and backrests with a damp cloth (plain water; mild, sauna-safe
  cleaner if needed — **no harsh chemicals**, they soak into bare wood).
- Sweep/mop the floor; lift and dry the duckboard.
- Check that vents move freely.

## 4. Seasonal / annual

- **Stones:** restack annually; **replace** any that have cracked or crumbled
  (degraded stones reduce löyly and airflow). Rinse new stones before use.
- **Benches:** sand lightly if rough; re-apply **sauna paraffin oil** to
  high-contact wood if used. **Never varnish/paint.**
- **Inspect** the heater elements, guard rail fasteners, door catch, and hinges.
- **Check the foil/wood** at penetrations for any moisture staining (a sign of a
  barrier leak — address promptly).
- **Electrical:** have the heater circuit/GFCI checked periodically; address any
  nuisance trips with the electrician rather than ignoring them.

## 5. Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---------|--------------|-----|
| Won't reach temperature | Undersized heater; insulation/barrier gaps; too much glass; vents too open | Verify kW vs. volume ([`06`](06-heater-sizing.md)); check foil continuity ([`05`](05-insulation-vapor-barrier.md)); tune vents ([`08`](08-ventilation.md)) |
| Heats but weak löyly | Too few/degraded stones; oversized heater not loading stones | Restack/replace stones; verify stone weight |
| Stuffy / not enough air | Intake blocked; exhaust closed | Open/clear vents; rebalance |
| Musty smell / staining | Room not drying after use; barrier leak | Always dry after use; inspect/seal foil |
| Resin beads on wood | A resinous board used inside | Replace that board with sauna-safe wood |
| Sensor/temp mismatch | Sensor mis-placed | Reposition sensor per manual ([`07`](07-electrical.md)) |
| GFCI/breaker trips | Moisture, fault, or undersized circuit | Stop use; call electrician ([`07`](07-electrical.md)) |
| Door won't stay shut / hard to open | Catch/hinge out of adjustment; wood movement | Adjust ball-catch/hinges; never add an outside lock |

## 6. Longevity habits (the short version)

1. **Dry the room after every use** (open vent + door ajar).
2. **No varnish, no plastic, no resinous wood** inside.
3. **Restack/replace stones** yearly.
4. **Keep ventilation working** and balanced.
5. **Fix moisture stains** at the barrier immediately.
